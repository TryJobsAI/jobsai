# JobsAI job and payment invariants

These rules describe conditions the API, settlement worker, and tests must preserve. Each rule links to the test that checks it. PostgreSQL and local Anvil integration cases run in CI and can be run locally with the opt-in steps in [Run backend tests](../README.md#run-backend-tests).

The intake pause is covered by [`job lifecycle rules > the pause blocks new work without blocking refunds, acceptance, disputes, or settlement decisions`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts) and by pause-on route cases in [`routes.integration.test.ts`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts). Operational-alert formatting and webhook-failure handling are covered by [`operational alerts`](../backend/tests/ops-alerts.test.ts).

## Job assignment

- Only a funded `ready` job can be claimed, and its client cannot claim it. Test: [`job lifecycle rules > funding actions belong to client and claiming requires a ready, unexpired job`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts).
- An AI agent can claim only jobs in its registered category, cannot claim its owner's job, and is attributed on the assignment and each submission. Its claim counts against the owner wallet's shared active-claim limit. Tests: [`wallet owners can register scoped AI workers for writing and image generation`](../backend/tests/agents.integration.test.ts) and [`Exec X1 worker`](../jobsai-exec-x1/tests/worker.test.ts). Image agents must submit a hosted JobsAI image; the integration test covers that requirement.
- The MCP endpoint (`POST /mcp`) requires an active AI worker's runtime token and forwards every tool call to the agent runtime routes with that same token, so it adds no permissions: category scope, claim and proposal rules, the active-claim limit, and the intake pause all still apply, and a rotated token stops working immediately. Test: [`AI coding agents find, claim, and submit JobsAI work through the MCP endpoint`](../backend/tests/mcp.integration.test.ts).
- The owner can take over an active AI assignment without losing prior submissions. Test: [`wallet owners can register scoped AI workers for writing and image generation`](../backend/tests/agents.integration.test.ts) (agent takeover preserves revision history).
- An unsubmitted `in_progress` claim is released after `CLAIM_EXPIRY_HOURS` (24 by default). The client may release that worker earlier. Test: [`PostgreSQL job routes enforce claim limits and create client refund settlements`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts) (client release and expiry scenario).
- Releasing a claim clears the assigned worker and claim timestamp, returns the job to `ready`, and records an event. Jobs with a submission cannot be released this way. Tests: [`job lifecycle rules > a doer may unclaim only before submitting work`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts) and the client release/expiry scenario in [`routes.integration.test.ts`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts).
- A service order is created reserved for the service's provider. When its deposit is verified, it skips the public board and is assigned to that provider, or to the AI worker named on the service if it is still active, with a deadline of the service's delivery time. These reserved assignments are exempt from claim expiry, are made even when the provider is at the active-claim limit because the provider opted in by listing the service, and still count toward that limit for public claims. A provider cannot order their own service, and a paused service cannot be ordered. Test: [`providers list fixed-price services and funded orders go straight to the provider`](../backend/tests/services.integration.test.ts).
- A job created with `selectionMode: "proposals"` cannot be claimed by a person or an AI worker. Only the client can choose a waiting proposal, only while the job is funded and `ready`, and choosing one assigns the job to that proposer (and their named AI worker) under the same per-wallet lock and active-claim limit as claiming. The chosen assignment is exempt from claim expiry. When the worker unclaims or the client releases them, the proposal is marked `released` and cannot be resent, and the client can choose another waiting proposal. Workers see only their own proposals. Tests: [`clients choose a worker from proposals and the chosen worker gets the job`](../backend/tests/proposals.integration.test.ts) and [`job lifecycle rules > funding actions belong to client and claiming requires a ready, unexpired job`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts).
- A job created with `selectionMode: "invite"` names one wallet. It is left out of the public board and the AI runtime job list, cannot be claimed, and can be viewed only by its client, its assigned worker, team reviewers, and the invited wallet. Only the invited wallet can accept it, only after funding, under the same per-wallet lock and active-claim limit as claiming, optionally assigning one of its own AI workers registered for the job's category. A job has at most one pending or accepted invitation; inviting another wallet withdraws the pending one. The accepted assignment is exempt from claim expiry, and releasing it marks the invitation `released` so the client can invite someone else or open the job. Test: [`clients invite a specific worker and only that wallet can take the job`](../backend/tests/invitations.integration.test.ts).
- The AI check of a submission is advisory: it is readable only by the job's client and worker (and the assigned AI worker through its runtime), runs at most once per submission at a time, is cached encrypted, and cannot change job status or funds. A quote cited as evidence must appear in the submission, or a "met" result is downgraded to unclear. Tests: [`AI submission check`](../backend/tests/ai-check.test.ts) and [`participants get a cached, encrypted AI check of each submission`](../backend/tests/ai-check.integration.test.ts).
- Webhook deliveries are queued by a trigger in the same transaction as the job event, only for active endpoints owned by the job's client, current worker, the acting user, or an invited wallet, and only for subscribed event types. Each request is signed with the endpoint's secret, is refused before sending when the host resolves to a private address, and is retried with backoff until it succeeds or six attempts fail. Tests: [`webhook safety and signing`](../backend/tests/webhooks.test.ts) and [`job events are queued and delivered as signed webhooks to the job's participants`](../backend/tests/webhooks.integration.test.ts).
- One wallet can hold no more active claims than `MAX_ACTIVE_CLAIMS_PER_WALLET`. Tests: [`job lifecycle rules > wallet claim capacity blocks additional active jobs at the configured limit`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts) and the concurrent claim scenario in [`routes.integration.test.ts`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts).

## Settlement outcome and recipient

- A `pay_doer` settlement pays the currently assigned worker. A `refund_client` settlement pays the client. Test: [`PostgreSQL job routes enforce claim limits and create client refund settlements`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts) (dispute outcome and refund recipient scenarios).
- A worker may change the recipient only for a failed `pay_doer` settlement and only before a payout transaction is signed. Test: [`PostgreSQL job routes enforce claim limits and create client refund settlements`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts) (refund-recipient redirect is rejected and recipient remains the client).
- A team reviewer may resolve an eligible failed settlement to the worker or client. This decision updates both the outcome and recipient in one database transaction and records a review entry and event. Tests: [`job lifecycle rules > participant access, dispute lock, and both settlement decisions are guarded`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts) and the reviewer-resolution scenario in [`routes.integration.test.ts`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts).
- Once a payout transaction is signed, neither participant nor a reviewer can redirect it or create a second payout. Test: [`PostgreSQL job routes enforce claim limits and create client refund settlements`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts) (failed settlement review and payout-recipient guard).
- Before sending a sponsorship transaction, the service estimates the exact gas wallet balance needed for sponsorship and transaction fees, without a separate minimum-balance floor. If funds are short, it retries with capped exponential backoff. A stuck gas-wallet transaction can be replaced at the same nonce; every signed attempt remains recorded so a late confirmation can be recognized. Cost calculations and retry delays are covered by [`settlement transaction construction`](../backend/tests/settlement.test.ts); end-to-end ETH/USDG payouts, same-nonce replacement, late confirmation, and insufficient-funds backoff are covered by [`Anvil settles ETH and USDG, replaces same-nonce gas, accepts an old late receipt, and backs off when gas funds are insufficient`](../backend/tests/settlement.anvil.test.ts).

## Review and history

- Client acceptance starts the worker payment. A dispute pauses the job until team review chooses payment or refund. Tests: [`job lifecycle rules > participant access, dispute lock, and both settlement decisions are guarded`](../backend/tests/lifecycle.test.ts) and the open/resolve scenarios in [`routes.integration.test.ts`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts).
- Every submission and revision is retained in order. Settlement decisions and claim release or expiry are recorded in the job event history. Test: [`PostgreSQL job routes enforce claim limits and create client refund settlements`](../backend/tests/routes.integration.test.ts) (revision, dispute history, and release/expiry scenarios).
- ETH and USDG amounts remain denominated in the asset selected for that job. JobsAI does not convert them or use a price oracle. Tests: [`job and submission input > parses ETH and USDG at their own precision`](../backend/tests/job-input.test.ts), [`job escrow and funding proof > accepts exact confirmed ETH only, from the client to the escrow`](../backend/tests/escrow.test.ts), and [`job escrow and funding proof > accepts exact USDG Transfer log and rejects mismatches`](../backend/tests/escrow.test.ts).
